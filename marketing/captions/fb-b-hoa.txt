For anyone running a residents' group or HOA page in New Providence:

You already do the work — someone always knows which road is flooded first. NowBey gives that knowledge somewhere to live. Residents drop a pin, neighbours confirm it, and everyone can see how fresh the information is, instead of scrolling back through a week of comments.

Two things that might matter to your members:
– Storm warnings are sent to everyone, free. No plan required.
– Old reports are labelled, not deleted, so the history stays visible.
