A fortnight in, so here's the honest version again for anyone who missed it 👇

NowBey is a map of what Nassau neighbours can actually see: flooding, road hazards, power cuts, water outages. You can see who reported it, and how long ago somebody confirmed it — which is the part that makes it useful and not just another group chat.

Free to look. No account. Works in the browser: nowbey-v2.vercel.app

If your area has a group, this is the thing to drop into it. A map is only as good as the people on it. 🌧️🗺️
