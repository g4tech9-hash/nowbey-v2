06 — RULES

The four that matter

Everything else is a preference. These are the ones that break the asset.

Keep type inside the safe circleOn the 1080 the coverage line sits at y = 0.792. Anything lower clips on a round crop.
Use the ladder, don't shrinkLoad the 128 and 64 exports. They drop the wordmark and enlarge the emblem.
Never put the 3D night master on lightIts bloom is baked for dark. Use the 3D light master or the UI set.
Gold is an accent, never a fieldNode dots and pin tip only. It is the one warm note in the system.
Rebuild everything with ./build.sh · assets in out/ui, out/png, out/svg
