## 1. Where Bahamians actually are

Spend effort in proportion to reach, not in proportion to how famous the app is globally.

| Tier | Channels | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **Facebook Page + WhatsApp** | Facebook is the default public square across the Caribbean, and WhatsApp groups are how neighbourhood information actually moves. A WhatsApp Channel (broadcast, one-way) is the single highest-leverage thing you can open. |
| **Secondary** | Instagram, TikTok | Younger audience; TikTok over-indexes for short map/storm clips that travel. |
| **Niche but valuable** | X, LinkedIn, YouTube, GitHub, Reddit | X for journalists, officials and NEMA-adjacent accounts. LinkedIn for Oasis Technology's credibility with partners. YouTube for short how-to clips. |
| **Not applicable** | Nextdoor | Not available in The Bahamas. Skip it despite being the obvious "neighbourhood" app. |

**Seasonality matters more than cadence.** Hurricane season runs 1 June – 30 November. Build the following *before* June and expect your highest-value posting to be storm coverage, not marketing.

## 5. Content pillars

Post only these four things. Anything else dilutes a safety product.

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Useful now** | 40% | "Power out on Shirley St East — confirmed by 6 neighbours." Live, specific, local. |
| **Teach** | 25% | How to drop a pin in two taps; how alerts work; what "confirmed" means. |
| **Proof** | 20% | Screenshots of the map working during a real event; a neighbour's report that helped someone. |
| **Build in public** | 15% | New islands coming, what shipped this week, honest limitations. |

**Never post during an active emergency as marketing.** During an event, post only verified,
useful information and official channels. A safety app that looks like it is profiting from a
storm loses the room permanently.

## 7. Community rules (pin these)

1. Report what you can see. Don't guess, don't repeat rumours.
2. No names, no photos of people's homes, no licence plates.
3. No advertising, no politics unrelated to safety.
4. Deliberately false reports get you removed.
5. **In an emergency, call 919 or 911.** NowBey is not an emergency service.

## 9. Measure

Early on, ignore follower counts. Track:

- **Reports filed per week** — the only number that proves value
- **Confirmations per report** — proves the community is real
- **Alerts sign-ups** and which islands they cover
- **Repeat reporters** — the signal that it's a habit, not a novelty
