"community safety map", "news wire", "hazard report", island and place names,
and the names of hazard categories (flooding, storm, power, water, fire).

Third-party marks belong to their owners and are used here only to identify
those services: **Google** (Play, Sign-In, Firebase, Maps), **Apple** (App
Store), **Stripe**, **Expo / EAS** (Expo Application Services), **Vercel**,

## Rules for any permitted use

If Oasis Technology grants written permission to use a mark:

1. Use it exactly as supplied — do not recolour, distort, re-proportion or add
   effects to the logo, and do not change the word mark's spelling or casing
   (`NowBey`, never `Nowbey`, `Now Bey` or `NOWBEY`).
2. Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "N".
3. Never use a mark in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership, or that
   Oasis Technology is the source of your product or content.
4. Never register or use a domain, app package name, social handle or store
   listing containing `nowbey`, or a misspelling or transliteration of it.
5. Include this notice: *"NowBey™ is a trademark of Oasis Technology."*

## Reporting infringement

Send to **oasistechnology242@gmail.com** with the subject "Trademark": the
infringing URL or store listing, screenshots, and your relationship to the
matter. For hosted copies, a GitHub DMCA notice or a Google Play IP complaint
is usually faster than correspondence — say the word and we will file it.

## Registration plan

The Bahamas is essentially first-to-file, so priority goes to whoever files
first rather than whoever used the name first. Sequence:

1. **Clearance search** — Bahamas Registrar General (Industrial Property
   Office) plus USPTO TESS, for class 9 (downloadable mobile applications),
   42 (SaaS / platform) and 38 (alert and notification services). Do this
   before any filings: the point is to find a conflict now, not after brand
   recognition exists.
2. **File in The Bahamas** — Registrar General's Department, Industrial
   Property Office, under the Trademarks Act.
3. **File with the USPTO** if there are US users or a US store listing.
4. **Record the registration** with the app stores so their takedown routes
   are available.
