Before you can publish

The publisher needs five values from your own accounts — a Meta token, the Facebook Page id, the Instagram Business id, optionally a TikTok token, and a public URL where the media is hosted. CREDENTIALS.md shows exactly where each one lives, click by click, including the Instagram-linking step that blocks most launches.
