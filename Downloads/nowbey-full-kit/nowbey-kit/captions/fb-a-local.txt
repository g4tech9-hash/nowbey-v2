Quick one for the Nassau folks 👇

Every time it rains hard, the same thing happens in the group: someone asks if Bay Street is passable, and forty people guess.

NowBey is a map of exactly that — neighbour reports for flooding, road hazards, power cuts and water. You can see who reported it and how recently somebody confirmed it, so you know whether to trust it.

It's free to look at, you don't need an account, and it works in the browser: nowbey.com

If you're in a group for your area, drop it in there. A map is only as good as the people on it. 🌧️🗺️
