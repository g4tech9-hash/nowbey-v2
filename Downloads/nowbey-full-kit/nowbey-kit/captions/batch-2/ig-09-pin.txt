You do not need to be an admin to warn your street. 📍

Four steps. Twenty seconds. One line of text.

Water across the lane on your way to work? Open NowBey. Pick what it is — flooding, a road hazard, a downed line, an accident. Drop the pin exactly where you are standing. Say what you can see. Send. Your name on it, or anonymous — your call. Lose signal halfway through? Your draft waits for you.

That is the whole flow. No form to fill in, no group chat to scroll back through, no admin to approve you.

And it does not end at your pin. Neighbours confirm it, or mark it cleared — so the person behind you can see whether water is still across the road, and how recently somebody actually checked.
