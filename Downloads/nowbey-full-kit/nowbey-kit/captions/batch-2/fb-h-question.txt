Genuine question for the Nassau folks, and we want the honest answers 👇

What is your routine before the rain mash up?

The gully gets checked. The car moves somewhere higher. The phone gets charged. Somebody's grandmother calls round to make sure everybody's good — and honestly, that is a whole early-warning system on one phone line.

Tell us yours. What do you check first? What do you do that your neighbours should be copying?

We are reading every comment, and we will put the best ones into a "what Nassau does before the rain" post — with your name on it, or anonymous if you would rather.

And if you want your street to know what you know: drop a pin on nowbey.com when the water starts rising, confirm a neighbour's report when you drive past, and set up free email alerts for New Providence. Storm warnings always go out, and they are always free.
