Somebody sends the link. Nobody says where it happened. 📰

That is the gap the NowBey news wire closes. We collect stories from Bahamian outlets in one place, and put each one on the map — so a story is not just "across the island", it is where it happened, with the source named and a link back to the outlet that reported it.

Every story is reviewed by a human before it publishes. Community-submitted links go through the same review, because a safety product cannot afford a viral rumour.

Read it alongside the hazard map and you get both halves: what the outlets are reporting, and what your neighbours are seeing on the ground.
