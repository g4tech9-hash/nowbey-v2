For the parents, the aunties and anybody who checks on everybody else 🌧️

We want to be careful here, because this matters.

Official alerts stay official. A MARCO Alert — the missing-child alert issued by the Royal Bahamas Police Force — reaches your phone through your carrier. Hurricane warnings come from NEMA and the Met Office. No app should tell you otherwise, and we are not going to. In an emergency, call 911 / 919.

What those alerts cannot tell you is whether your own lane is passable at 7:40 in the morning. That is neighbour knowledge, and it is the part we built NowBey for.

Two things worth knowing if you run a household:
– Email alerts are free. Pick your island, pick what you want to hear about — flooding, road hazards, power. One profile per email address.
– Storm warnings are sent to everyone, on every plan, for free. No plan required, ever.

Email is the channel that works today. Text-message alerts and iPhone notifications are not built yet, and we would rather say that than pretend otherwise.

Set yours up: nowbey.com/alerts — live for New Providence.
