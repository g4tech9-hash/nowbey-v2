NowBey collects stories from Bahamian outlets in one place — and puts each one on the map, so a story is not just "across the island", it is where it happened. Heavy rain floods low-lying streets. Every story is reviewed by a human before it publishes. Sources are named on every card. Community-submitted links go through the same review, because a safety product cannot afford a viral rumour — and because your trust is the only thing this wire has going for it.

Read it next to the hazard map and you get the two halves of the same picture: what the outlets are reporting, and what your neighbours are actually seeing on the ground right now.
