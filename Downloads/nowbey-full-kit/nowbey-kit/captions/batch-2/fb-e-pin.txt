The fastest way to warn your street is not the group chat. 📍

Every hard rain, the same thread: somebody asks if a road is passable, and forty people guess. NowBey is four steps instead — pick what it is, drop the pin where you are standing, one line about what you can see, send. Your name or anonymous.

Then neighbours confirm it or mark it cleared, so the next driver is not reading last week's comments to work out whether the water has gone down.

Free to look at, no account needed, works in the browser: nowbey.com/report

Live for New Providence today. If you are in a group for your area, drop it in there — a map is only as good as the people on it. 🌧️
